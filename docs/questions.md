# Every question we ask

Generated from `lib/schema.ts` — do not edit by hand. Run `npm run questions` to rebuild after changing the questions.

Two separate forms, sent weeks apart. **Tell us about your clinic** goes out the day a client signs — 16 questions, about five minutes. **Connecting your accounts** goes out after the kickoff call, once there is enough trust to ask for logins.

Business types: *Clinic or multi-practitioner practice*, *Solo practitioner*, *Personal trainer or coach*, *Gym, studio or class-based*, *Something else*.

Everything deliberately left out of these forms lives in the master discovery checklist — those are questions for the kickoff conversation, or things Clinic Evo researches itself.

### Access checklist

For each item the client picks one of: **Done**, **Will do**, **Do not have one**, **Not sure — help me**.

**Your domain name**

Whoever you bought the domain from — often GoDaddy, 123-reg or Namecheap. They all have a way to add a second person. If you are not sure who it is with, say so and we will find out.

**Web hosting**

The company the website actually sits on, which is sometimes the same as the domain. Most allow a second login.

**Your website login**

In WordPress: Users, Add New, set the role to Administrator. Squarespace, Wix and Webflow all have an equivalent invite.

**Google Business Profile**

The listing that shows on Google Maps. Business Profile, Settings, People and access, Add, then Manager.

**Google Analytics**

Admin, Property access management, add our email as Editor. Plenty of clinics do not have this set up — that is fine, we will create it.

**Google Search Console**

Settings, Users and permissions, Add user, Full. Same as above — if it does not exist we will set it up.

**Facebook and Instagram**

Meta Business Suite, Settings, People, invite our email with Content access. This is your pages, not advertising.

**Booking system**

Add us as a staff or admin user, so we can connect booking to the new site.

**Google Tag Manager**

> Only if they run paid ads.

Admin, User Management, the + button, then Publish permission. Only relevant if someone set this up for you previously.

**Google Ads**

> Sent to **dmorgan18@googlemail.com**, not the address given above.

> Only if they run paid ads.

Admin, Access and security, the + button, enter the email and choose Admin. Google sends an invitation that needs accepting, so it will not show as active immediately.

**Meta Ads account**

> Sent to **dmorgan18@googlemail.com**, not the address given above.

> Only if they run paid ads.

Meta Business Suite, Settings, Ad accounts, pick the account, Add people, then Manage campaigns. Separate from your pages above.

**TikTok Ads**

TikTok Ads Manager, Assets, Users, Invite, choose Admin.

**Email marketing**

Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Brevo and similar all let you invite a second user.

**Payments**

Stripe, Settings, Team, invite as Developer.
